Dr Segawa Asadu

Company Secretary

Dr Segawa serves as Vexovin's Company Secretary, keeping the company's governance, statutory filings and board processes in good order.

As Company Secretary, Dr Segawa advises the board on governance and its legal duties, makes sure Vexovin meets its obligations under Uganda's Companies Act and to the Uganda Registration Services Bureau (URSB), and keeps the company's statutory registers, resolutions and records up to date.

The role also covers coordinating board and shareholder meetings, from notices and agendas through to minutes, so that decisions are properly made, recorded and followed through.

  • Corporate governance
  • Company secretarial practice
  • Regulatory compliance
  • Board administration
