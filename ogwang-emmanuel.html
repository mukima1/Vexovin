Ogwang Emmanuel

Executive Chairman

Emmanuel chairs Vexovin's board, guiding the company's governance, long-term direction and key partnerships.

He is a director at Oyam Institute of Sciences and Technology, where he provides leadership in education and skills development. His experience leading an academic institution gives Vexovin valuable insight into the needs of schools and learners — insight that directly informs products such as Attendify.

He brings a steady, people-centred leadership style and a commitment to building institutions that serve their communities well.
