Ssenkima Ashiraf

CEO & Founder

Ashiraf founded Vexovin to turn ideas into practical technology that schools, businesses and communities can rely on every day. As Chief Executive Officer he sets the company's vision and leads product strategy and engineering across the Vexovin portfolio.

He brings strong technical expertise in software engineering, AI automation and digital platform design, together with hands-on experience taking innovations from concept to working products used by real customers.

Ashiraf holds a Master's degree in Technology, Innovation and Industrial Development from Makerere University, which shapes Vexovin's focus on technology that drives productivity and industrial growth in Uganda and beyond.
