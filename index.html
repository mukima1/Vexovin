FAQ

Common questions

What kind of projects does Vexovin take on?

Web platforms, Android and cross-platform apps, school management systems, SaaS products, API integrations and AI automation. If technology can solve your problem, we are happy to talk it through.

Is Vexovin a registered company?

Yes. Vexovin is incorporated in Uganda with the Uganda Registration Services Bureau (URSB) and is certified by the Personal Data Protection Office (PDPO) under the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019.

Do you work with clients outside Uganda?

Yes. We are based in Kampala and work remotely with schools, startups and businesses anywhere.

How can my school start using Attendify?

Visit attendify.site or request a demo below and choose “Attendify — school system” as the topic.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. We offer maintenance, updates and continued development so your product keeps improving after it goes live.
