Cookie Policy

How Vexovin uses cookies and similar technologies to make our products and website work better for you.

Effective date: 1 January 2025 Last updated: May 2025
This Cookie Policy applies to vexovin.com and all web-based Vexovin products. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website when you visit it. They allow the site to remember information about your visit — such as your preferences or login state — making your next visit easier and the site more useful to you.

Similar technologies such as web beacons, pixel tags, local storage, and session storage may also be used for related purposes, and references to "cookies" in this policy include these technologies unless stated otherwise.

2. Categories of Cookies We Use

These cookies are essential for our services to function. They cannot be switched off in our systems. They are typically set in response to actions you take, such as logging in or filling in forms.

These cookies collect information about how our pages are performing — for example, whether pages load quickly or whether users encounter errors.

2.5 Marketing / Targeting Cookies

We currently use limited marketing cookies. Where we do, they are used to display relevant content and measure the effectiveness of our promotional activities. We do not use marketing cookies to build third-party advertising profiles.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies on our platforms are placed by third-party services. We do not control these cookies — please refer to the respective third-party privacy policies for more information:

  • Google Analytics — analytics and performance measurement
  • Firebase / Google — authentication and real-time database services used by Attendify and Postgraming
  • Formspree — contact form submission processing
  • Google Fonts — font delivery (may set performance cookies)

4. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can control and manage cookies in several ways:

  • Browser settings: Most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies. Refer to your browser's help documentation for instructions (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
  • Opt-out tools: For Google Analytics, you can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on
  • Device settings: Mobile devices often provide options to limit ad tracking in system settings

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our services. Strictly necessary cookies cannot be disabled as they are required for core functionality.

5. Do Not Track

Some browsers have a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature that signals to websites that you do not want to be tracked. Our analytics services may not currently respond to DNT signals. We are monitoring developments in this area and will update our practices accordingly.

6. Cookie Retention

Cookies are retained for the durations specified in the tables above. Session cookies are deleted when you close your browser. Persistent cookies remain on your device until they expire or until you delete them.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy as our technology or legal requirements change. We will post the updated policy on this page with a revised effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
